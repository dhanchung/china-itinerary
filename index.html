Eight-day Guangzhou, Shenzhen and Hong Kong family itinerary from 21 to 28 November, one tab per day.

Sat 21 NovGuangzhou

Morning

Arrive Guangzhou

Metro Line 3 then Line 1 to Gongyuanqian; check in at Zhonglv Commercial City.

Afternoon

Beijing Road

Pedestrian street on your doorstep; snacks and early dinner.

Evening

Canton Tower

Line 1 to Tiyu Xilu, then Line 3 to Canton Tower.

Sun 22 NovGuangzhou

8:00 AM

Dim sum in Liwan

Go early to beat the weekend queue.

10:00 AM

Chen Clan Ancestral Hall

Two stops west on Line 1.

Afternoon

Yongqing Fang and Enning Road

Line 1 to Changshou Lu; old lanes and street snacks.

Late afternoon

Shamian Island

Line 1 to Huangsha; colonial-era streets.

Evening

Pearl River night cruise

Board at Tianzi Wharf, a short walk from the hotel.

Mon 23 NovGuangzhou

8:30 AM

Head to Panyu

Line 1 to Tiyu Xilu, then Line 3 to Hanxi Changlong, about 45 min.

9:30 AM

Chimelong Safari Park

Pandas, koalas, white tigers and the tram through the free-roaming zone.

Evening

Chimelong International Circus

Acrobatic show next door; book ahead. Didi back, about 40 min.

Tue 24 NovGuangzhou

Morning

Yuexiu Park and Five Rams

Two stops north on Line 2; Zhenhai Tower and big green spaces.

Late morning

Nanyue King Mausoleum Museum

Next to Yuexiu Park; 2,000-year-old tomb with a jade burial suit.

Afternoon

Guangdong Museum

Near Canton Tower; regional history and Chaozhou woodcarving.

Evening

Huifu East Road food street

Last Guangzhou night: street food near the hotel.

Wed 25 NovShenzhen

Morning

Train to Shenzhen

Line 2 to Guangzhou South, train to Futian, then Line 2 to Huaqiangbei.

Midday

Check in

Drop bags at the hotel on Zhenhua Road.

Afternoon

Huaqiangbei

Short walk from the hotel; malls close around early evening.

Evening

Ping An Finance Centre

Night view from Shenzhen's tallest tower. Fill in new China arrival cards tonight.

Thu 26 NovHong Kong day trip

8:30 AM

High-speed train to West Kowloon

From Futian station, under 20 min. Both immigration checks are inside West Kowloon station; bring passports.

Morning

West Kowloon Cultural District

Next to the station; Hong Kong Palace Museum or M+ if you like museums.

Midday

Star Ferry and Victoria Peak

Cross the harbour to Central, then the Peak Tram up for the classic view.

Afternoon

Mong Kok

Sneakers Street, markets and street snacks.

Evening

Tsim Sha Tsui promenade

Harbour lights, then the train back to Futian. Book the return in advance and check the last train.

Fri 27 NovShenzhen

10:00 AM

Happy Valley Shenzhen

Line 2 direct to Window of the World, about 25 min; weekday queues are short.

Evening

Dongmen Pedestrian Street

Busy shopping street with street food and cheap buys.

Sat 28 NovShenzhen

Morning

Lianhuashan Park

About 10 min by Didi; view over the Futian skyline.

Midday

Lunch and check out

Chaoshan beef hotpot or dim sum near the hotel.

Afternoon

Fly home from Shenzhen

Didi to SZX, 40 to 50 min; allow extra time on a Saturday.
